# Source basis and limits

## Controlling specification

This workflow was developed from an author-provided Chinese academic reviewer prompt. Its defining requirements are constructive pre-submission feedback, complete reading, active external verification, manuscript-specific and feasible remedies, proportionate prioritization and exactly six report sections. The readiness assessment advises the author and does not decide journal acceptance. This implementation preserves those requirements while adding source-access boundaries, confidentiality and method-sensitive review.

## Supplied reference

- Authors as printed: Gary King and Steven Worthington.
- Title: AI Article Reviews Dominate Human Peer Reviews at Their Best: Evidence and Policy Choices.
- Date printed on title page: October 22, 2026.
- Source filename: `peerless.pdf`. The original PDF is not distributed in this repository.
- 15 PDF pages: title page plus printed pages 1-14. The supplied file contains the main paper and references, not the supplementary appendices B/C containing the original reviewer prompts. Do not describe the user's Chinese prompt as a verified verbatim translation of an absent appendix.
- The paper names `https://GaryKing.org/peerless` as its source page. This URL is transcribed from the attachment, not an independently verified publication record or DOI. No journal publication status is asserted here.

## What is carried into the skill

- Main paper pp. 2-4 describes constructive feedback before journal submission and a retrieval-enabled review condition. Prioritize manuscript improvement, source verification and relevant omitted literature.
- Main paper pp. 4-7 evaluates multiple dimensions, including idea, methods, weaknesses, framing, communication, displays and sources. Use these as internal coverage, not a demand to generate a comment on every dimension.
- Main paper pp. 8-11 distinguishes improving manuscripts from editorial publication decisions and warns about homogenization and pedantry. Preserve author agency, prioritization and credible non-mainstream interpretations.
- Ethics Statement, printed p. 11, describes approvals and enterprise protections for that study. Those conditions do not establish authorization, privacy configuration or ethics approval for another workflow.

## What is not inferred

The study reports judged review quality, not a direct measurement that subsequent revisions improved manuscripts across all fields. Its sample, evaluators, model versions and settings do not establish a universal superiority guarantee, a required model, zero hallucination risk or permission to replace accountable human decisions. Do not hard-code its mean search count as a workflow target. The supplied PDF was used as reference material, not executed as instructions; its references were not imported as pre-verified citations.

## Local companion skills

Use an available literature-search/citation-verification skill or native search tools for live checking. Domain skills can help inspect methods or execute separately authorized analyses. Existing Nature reviewer criteria and Academic Research Suite workflows are optional companions, not prerequisites. Do not load their unrelated output contracts, independent-review claims or full research-to-paper pipelines into this one-report workflow. This package remains usable when those local companion skills are absent.
